The air has changed in one lifetime

Direct measurements at Mauna Loa, Hawaii started in 1958. The yearly wiggle is plants breathing in and out. The climb is us. Before industry, CO₂ sat near 280 parts per million.

Where emissions come from

Greenhouse gases come from several places, not one. Power and heat make up the biggest slice, which is also where solar, wind and batteries are growing fastest.

What actually moves the number

Clean electricity first. It is the biggest single lever, and it cleans up cars, heating and industry as they plug in.

Waste less energy. Insulation, public transport and less food waste cut emissions and often save money.

Protect forests and wetlands. They store carbon and cool their regions. Keeping them is cheaper than replacing them.

Keep talking about it. The biggest choices belong to governments and companies. Voting and asking questions keeps the pressure on.
